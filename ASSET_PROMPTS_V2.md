# ImageGen — prompts v2

Outil intégré ImageGen. Une génération séparée par personnage, puis une pose de balayage séparée par personnage. PNG transparents enregistrés dans `dist/assets/sprites/`.
+
## v2 — jin

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: ONE CHARACTER production fighting game animation sprite sheet. Input image: costume reference ONLY; create a new sheet of only the requested fighter, never a multi-character roster.
Output: transparent PNG, square 1024x1024. EXACTLY 4 columns x 4 rows of equally sized 256x256 cells. Precisely one complete sprite per cell. All sprites face RIGHT, same head size, same body proportions, same costume details, exactly two arms and two legs, well-defined hands (closed fists where possible) and identical hair design. Sixteen full-body frames. Every cell has transparent padding on all four sides. Every standing sprite's feet baseline at local y=236. Sprites never touch their cell border, never overlap other cells. Full fist and kick extensions within local x=16..240. Background genuinely transparent; no shadows, no lettering, no labels or dividers.
Style: authentic manually constructed arcade pixel animation, like meticulously edited late 1990s Neo Geo fighters. Effective sprite resolution about 88 x 116 pixels enlarged with nearest-neighbor blocks, one consistent integer pixel grid. Clear compact pixel clusters, clean dark 1-pixel outline, 4 tones per material, about 24 colors total, readable skin and textile highlights. No smooth shading, no pseudo pixel texture over paintings, no glossy AI rendering, no soft gradients, no glows, no inconsistent details. Deliberate repeatable shape language and clean animation continuity. All frames show the SAME person.
Grid frame sequence in strict row-major order:
Row 1: 0 idle guard; 1 idle breathing guard slight weight shift; 2 walking first step; 3 walking opposite step.
Row 2: 4 jab anticipation fist drawn back; 5 fully extended straight jab; 6 jab recovery; 7 fully extended forward side kick.
Row 3: 8 launcher crouched windup; 9 rising uppercut; 10 crouch guard with visible arms; 11 standing block forearms raised.
Row 4: 12 hit recoil; 13 knocked down on floor complete full body within cell; 14 victory one fist raised; 15 powerful forward rage strike with both legs grounded.
Prioritize anatomical accuracy, sprite integrity and character continuity over decorative detail. No missing hands, no extra fingers or limbs, no broken knees or bent bones, no fused clothing, no sliced boots.
Subject: Jin Kazama. Athletic adult man, angular jaw, natural human face with exactly two eyes and one nose, black spiky hair swept up. Black red cropped combat jacket over bare chest, black trousers with restrained red flame motifs, red armored gloves, black boots. Row 1 of the reference image is costume guidance.
