## Atlas des combattants

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production sprite atlas for a 2D pixel art fighting game inspired by Tekken. Generate a transparent PNG sprite sheet, exactly SIX columns and FOUR rows, all 24 cells equal size, no dividers or text. Each cell contains one full body fighter with feet on same baseline and safe transparent padding. Each row is the SAME character in six poses facing RIGHT: column 1 fighting idle guard, column 2 stepping forward, column 3 extended straight punch, column 4 extended high side kick, column 5 recoiling from impact, column 6 triumphant fist raised. Row 1 Jin Kazama black spiky hair, black red combat jacket, dark trousers and red gauntlets. Row 2 Kazuya Mishima swept black hair, purple long coat, gray trousers, purple electric gauntlets. Row 3 Jun Kazama long black hair, white pale blue asymmetrical dress coat, black leggings, black boots. Row 4 Reina short purple black bob, oversized purple black jacket, black shorts and purple boots. Style: exquisite authentic hand placed chunky arcade pixel art, 1990s SNK sprite aesthetic, hard square pixel edges, limited palettes, detailed silhouettes, consistent fighter scale, no smooth gradients, no 3D, no antialiasing. All bodies fully within their own cells, no overlapping adjacent cells, transparent background. Landscape atlas 3:2 ratio.

## Arène Moonlit Shrine

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: seamless full-width background arena for a 2D fighting game. A nighttime Japanese shrine courtyard in heavy rain, dramatic central red torii gate, distant violet mountains, moon behind storm clouds, dark cherry trees framing the sides, warm red lanterns, neon magenta reflections, weathered stone fighting platform occupying lower 25 percent. Perfect side-view fighting stage: horizontal ground plane, unobstructed central space, dramatic layered parallax composition. Exquisite authentic 16-bit pixel art, visible square pixels, rich dark purple and blue with vivid crimson accents, cinematic lighting, crisp retro arcade environment. Wide 16:9 composition. No fighters, no humans, no lettering, no UI, no logos.
